About Us

Busan Student Guide is made by international students, for international students. We know how confusing the first months in a new country can be, so we write down what we wish we had known.

Our goals

  • Explain important topics in simple language.
  • Keep information current and honest.
  • Help students find events and community in Busan.

Who runs this site

A small team of students studying in Busan. We are not a government office or a university department, so please verify official details with the proper offices.
