About student life in Busan

Busan is South Korea's second-largest city, with beaches, mountains, and many universities. Students come from all over the world and often ask the same questions in their first months. This site collects those answers in one place, written by students who have been through it.

Places every student should know

Haeundae Beach

Busan's most famous beach, easy to reach by subway. Busy in summer, calm in autumn.

Gamcheon Culture Village

A hillside neighbourhood of colourful houses, small galleries, and street art.

Gwangalli and Gwangan Bridge

A popular evening spot with cafes and a view of the lit-up bridge.
